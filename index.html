A FEW QUICK ANSWERS

Before you
pull in.

What does Gold include?

Everything in Silver, plus seat shampoo & extraction, carpet & floor shampoo, spot treatment and deep surface cleaning. Gold is the deeper-clean option shown above; Silver is a vacuum-and-wipe-down refresh.

Will my Gold detail cost $179.99?

Gold starts at $179.99 CAD before tax after your $25 credit. Vehicle size and condition affect the final price. Staff confirm your price before booking. Heavy staining or excessive pet hair may cost extra; staff confirm any additional charges before service begins.

Does claiming my credit book an appointment?

No. This form claims your credit. You still need to book an available appointment through Square or contact Onyx for help. If you already have an appointment, keep it; claiming does not change your booking.

Can returning customers use the credit?

Yes. The offer is available to both new and returning customers, once per customer.

Does every service over $100 qualify?

No. The $25 credit applies to Interior Silver, Interior Gold, Interior Platinum, Gloss Boost Polish, and window tint packages (Front 2, Rear 3, or All) when that package is priced at $100 or more. Staff confirm eligibility and your final price before booking. Ends Sunday, Sept 27.

Why does Square show the regular price?

Square shows the regular service price before this promotion. No upfront payment is required. Show your coupon to Onyx staff after your service and before paying: they will apply the $25 discount to an eligible service at the shop. The discount is not applied online.

What if I don’t know which service to choose?

Select “Not Sure — Help Me Choose” in the form. We’ll help you find an option for your vehicle’s condition and what you’d like done.
